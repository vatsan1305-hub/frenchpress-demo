How to Brew

Four minutes, four steps, zero shortcuts.

  1. ⚖️

    Grind & measure

    Add 30 g of coarse-ground coffee for every 500 ml of water.

  2. 💧

    Pour & bloom

    Pour water just off the boil (93°C) to wet the grounds, then wait 30 seconds.

  3. ⏱️

    Steep

    Fill to the top, place the lid, and let it steep for 4 minutes.

  4. 🌸

    Press & pour

    Press the plunger down slowly and serve right away.
